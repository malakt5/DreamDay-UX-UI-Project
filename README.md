# 💍 DreamDay — Wedding Planning App

**DreamDay** is a UX/UI project focused on designing an all-in-one wedding planning application that helps users organize their wedding with a simpler, more structured, and less stressful experience.

The project combines **UX research, user analysis, personas, ideation, sketching, and UI/UX design** to address common challenges faced during wedding planning.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Planning a wedding involves managing budgets, comparing options, finding reliable vendors, and coordinating multiple services.

DreamDay was designed to address these challenges through a centralized platform that brings essential wedding-planning services and tools together.

The proposed solution focuses on helping users:

* 💰 Manage their wedding budget
* ⏰ Organize their planning process
* 🔎 Find trusted vendors
* ⚖️ Compare different options
* ⭐ Use reviews and feedback when making decisions
* 📋 Manage wedding-related services in one place

## 🔎 UX Research

The project began with user research designed to understand users' needs, behaviors, expectations, and pain points related to wedding planning.

A survey was conducted with **10 participants**, and the results were analyzed through both quantitative and qualitative research.

### Key Findings

The research identified several important challenges:

* **Finding trusted vendors** was one of the biggest challenges.
* **Budget management** was another major concern.
* Users also experienced difficulties when **comparing different options**.
* Reviews and feedback have an important influence on users' decisions.
* Users showed interest in having a platform that provides the essential wedding-planning services in one place.

## 👤 User Personas

Based on the research findings, user personas were developed to represent different types of potential DreamDay users.

The personas helped translate research findings into user-centered design decisions.

## ✏️ Ideation & Sketching

The design process included sketching and exploring ideas for the application's structure and user experience before developing the proposed interface.

## 🎨 UX/UI Design

DreamDay's design focuses on creating a simple and organized wedding-planning experience.

The proposed application brings together services such as:

* 📸 Photographers
* 🏛️ Venues
* 🍽️ Caterers
* 🌸 Decorators
* 🎵 Entertainers

The final concept is centered around an **all-in-one planning dashboard**, with verified vendors and comparison tools to make the planning process easier and help users make more confident decisions.
